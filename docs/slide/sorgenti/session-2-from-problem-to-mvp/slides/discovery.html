Session 2 · From problem to MVP

03

Steve Blank’s customer development (The Four Steps to the Epiphany, 2005; later The Startup Owner’s Manual with Bob Dorf, 2012) puts the search for customers alongside product development. The four steps: customer discovery (find out whether the problem exists and who has it), customer validation (show that someone buys and that the sale repeats), customer creation (create demand at scale), company building (move from an organization that searches to a company that executes). The first two are the “search,” the last two the “execution”; if validation fails, you go back to discovery. Blank’s motto is “there are no facts inside the building”: inside the building there are only opinions. Question for the class: how many conversations with real customers did you have before choosing your idea?
