Session 2 · From problem to MVP

06

Three kinds of MVP that Ries describes in The Lean Startup. Dropbox: reliable file syncing was hard to build, so Drew Houston first showed in a video how it would work; by Houston’s account the beta waitlist went from about 5,000 to 75,000 sign-ups overnight (sources date the video anywhere between 2007 and 2008). Zappos: in 1999 Swinmurn tests whether people will buy shoes online without any inventory, photographing pairs in stores and buying them at full price for every order, often at a loss. It is a “Wizard of Oz” MVP: the customer sees an automated service, and behind it there is a person. Food on the Table (Austin): a “concierge” MVP, where the founders personally serve the first customer and add customers and automation only once they understand what works. Question for the class: which of these three MVPs could you run yourselves, this week, for less than €500?
