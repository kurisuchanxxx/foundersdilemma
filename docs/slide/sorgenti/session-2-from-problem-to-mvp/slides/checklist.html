Lesson

Summary

Before you build, five questions.

01

What is the riskiest hypothesis, the one that brings everything down if it’s false?

02

What is the cheapest way to test it?

03

Which number will tell us whether we were right, and what threshold did we set?

04

Have we talked to our first customers in person?

05

What will we do if the answer is no: pivot or persevere?

Session 2 · From problem to MVP

11

The five questions sum up the lesson and apply to any startup: the riskiest hypothesis (Ries), the cheapest experiment (the MVP), a metric chosen before looking at the data (so you don’t read only what confirms your view), direct contact with customers (Blank), and the choice between pivoting and persevering. Ask each team to answer in writing before round 2: the answers become the Hypotheses section of the decision memo.
