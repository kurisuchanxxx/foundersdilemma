One question to measure fit.

“How would you feel if you could no longer use the product?”

Very disappointed, somewhat disappointed, not disappointed. If at least 40% answer very disappointed, you have fit.

Session 2 · From problem to MVP

08

The test comes from Sean Ellis, who led early marketing and growth at Dropbox, LogMeIn and Eventbrite: you ask active users how they would feel if they could no longer use the product. Ellis observed that companies that went on to grow well were above 40% “very disappointed”; it is a leading indicator, not final proof. Rahul Vohra, founder of Superhuman (an email app), told the story on First Round Review and on the company blog: he started at 22% and brought the figure to 58% in about a year. He segmented his users, worked out what the most enthusiastic ones loved and what was holding back the undecided ones, and gave half of the roadmap to each of the two. Question for the class: who would you ask, all users or only active ones, and why?
