Data from the reference balancing run documented in the simulation model: 60 games with 6 bot companies each. Lean bots do deep discovery, build for quality and spend little; blitz bots launch early and spend heavily from the start. The blitz bots raise more and grow fast at first, but they never reach PMF: when the market reaches the early majority they fall into the chasm, get diluted more, and leave their founders far less wealth.
