Product-market fit

“Product/market fit means being in a good market with a product that can satisfy that market.”

Marc Andreessen, The only thing that matters (2007)

When it’s missing

Customers don’t see the value, word of mouth never takes off, sales drag on for months.

When it’s there

Customers buy faster than you can make the product and serve them.

In the game. Fit is a score from 0 to 100: problem clarity, solution fit, market pull, learning velocity.

Session 2 · From problem to MVP

07

The definition comes from Marc Andreessen, in the post The only thing that matters (June 2007, part of the PMarca Guide to Startups series), where he ties it to the Andy Rachleff line seen in session 1. Andreessen adds that you can always feel whether fit is happening: when it isn’t, customers don’t quite get the value, word of mouth isn’t spreading, usage isn’t growing that fast, reviews are lukewarm, and sales cycles take too long; when it is, customers buy the product just as fast as you can make it, and you hire people just to keep up with demand. For Andreessen, a startup’s life divides into before and after product-market fit. Question for the class: what signals would you see in your numbers if fit arrived?
