Lean startup: MVP, customer interviews, smoke tests, pivots. Product-market fit: a PMF score that grows with problem clarity, quality and traction, plus the Sean Ellis test. Diffusion of innovation: Rogers’ segments, the chasm, the beachhead. Unit economics: CAC, LTV, churn, payback, margin, elasticity. Finance: grants, loans, crowdfunding, SAFEs, priced rounds, option pool, waterfall. Team: archetypes, equity split, vesting and cliff. The founder’s dilemma: twenty-seven dilemmas from real cases, board control, the Rich or King ranking. Strategy: competitors that react, partnerships and acquisitions between teams. Ethics: dark patterns, customer data, promises to investors.
