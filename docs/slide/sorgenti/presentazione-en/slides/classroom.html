Founder’s Dilemma

09

Three ways to play. Solo vs bots is for individual study, homework and the practice game before the course. Hot-seat uses one computer or the projector: a privacy screen hides each team’s data until the team opens it, and the instructor advances the rounds from a PIN-protected console. Classroom with files: the instructor shares the game file, each team sends back a small decision file, the instructor imports them and advances the round. Bots fill the market when teams are few.
