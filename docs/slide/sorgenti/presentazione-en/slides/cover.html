Founder’s Dilemma

Build a startup.
Face the founder’s dilemma.

A startup grows like a flower, from seed to exit: an idea, the first customers, capital at the right time, the choices that make it bloom or wither.

kurisuchanxxx.github.io/foundersdilemma

Founder’s Dilemma is a startup business game for university classrooms. Each team founds a company and steers it round after round: product, market, funding, team and the dilemmas every founder meets. It runs in the browser for free, and as an app for macOS, Windows and Linux.
