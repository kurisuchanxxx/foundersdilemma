What you learn

Every mechanic
is a course concept.

The game makes tangible what stays abstract in lectures. Every system points to an entry in the app’s Learn section, with formulas and references.

The point for a course: students do not read about product-market fit, dilution or the chasm, they live them in their own company. Every mechanic in the game has a matching entry in the Learn section of the app, with formulas and references, so the debriefing can go straight from a number on screen to the theory.
