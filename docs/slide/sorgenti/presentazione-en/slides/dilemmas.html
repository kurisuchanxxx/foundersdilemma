The dilemmas are drawn from Wasserman’s research on founders and from startup ethics cases. Other examples: a client paying in 120 days, moving the company to Delaware. There is no right answer: each choice moves cash, morale, reputation and control, often several rounds later, and each one points to a course concept for the debriefing.
