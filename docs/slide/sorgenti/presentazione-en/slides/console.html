Founder’s Dilemma

10

The console gives the instructor a round timer and a projector view for the whole class, surprise events to inject into the market at any time, discussion prompts for each round’s debriefing and CSV exports of history, ranking and decisions. Same seed, same market: two classes can play exactly the same game. At the end, the Rich or King chart puts every team on one picture: to the right of the line founders keep the majority of votes, higher up their stake is worth more. Few teams manage both: it is Wasserman’s founder’s dilemma, measured. Each team also gets a printable report with a post-mortem.
