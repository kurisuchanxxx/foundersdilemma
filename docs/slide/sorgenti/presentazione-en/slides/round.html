Founder’s Dilemma

04

Decide, observe, discuss, revise: the loop follows the experiential learning model. Teams set budgets, price and model, go-to-market channel, hiring, investor pitches and the round’s dilemma. The engine is deterministic: with the same seed a game repeats identically, so two classes can be compared. XP, challenges and cups keep attention high between rounds, and the instructor console suggests discussion prompts.
