The game uses the numbers of a real startup: customers, revenue, cash, CAC, LTV, churn, payback, dilution and valuation. Eight sectors with different economics, external competitors that ship versions, close rounds and react, and four go-to-market channels. Mistakes are cheap here, and that is the point: students can fail, over-dilute or lose the board and learn from it.
