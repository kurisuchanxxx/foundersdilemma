Session 3 · Go-to-market and unit economics

07

An example with made-up numbers, to redo on the board. With 4% monthly churn a customer stays 25 months on average; each month they leave €40 of margin (€50 × 80%), so LTV is €1,000 and the €300 CAC is recovered in 7.5 months. Then change one number at a time and ask what happens: with 8% churn, lifetime drops to 12.5 months, LTV to €500 and the LTV/CAC ratio to 1.7, below the threshold. Churn is often the most powerful lever.
