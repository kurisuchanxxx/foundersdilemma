Session 3 · Go-to-market and unit economics

08

Everett Rogers’ curve splits the adopters of an innovation into five groups, with the classic shares of 2.5%, 13.5%, 34%, 34% and 16%. Each group buys for different reasons: innovators out of curiosity, early adopters to gain an edge before everyone else, the early majority once the solution is proven and others like them are using it. For a startup the consequence is practical: the first customers are not a sample of the market, and what convinces them is not enough to convince the ones who come next.
