Session 3 · Go-to-market and unit economics

10

Facebook did not launch for everyone: thefacebook.com opened on February 4, 2004 for Harvard students only, expanded in March to Stanford, Columbia and Yale and then to other universities, reached high schools in September 2005, and in September 2006 opened to anyone aged 13 or older with an email address. Each campus was a small, dense segment where the product became indispensable because all your classmates were on it: Moore’s beachhead in practice. Question for the class: what would have been the risk of opening to everyone on day one?
