Lesson

Summary

Five questions before you spend on marketing.

01

What is the product worth to the customer, and how do we know?

02

Which pricing model tracks the value the customer receives?

03

Does each customer bring in at least three times what it costs to acquire them?

04

Who are our early adopters, and who comes after them?

05

Which channel have we tested, and which one really works?

Session 3 · Go-to-market and unit economics

12

The five questions sum up the lesson and apply to any real startup: value, pricing model, unit economics, adoption and channel. Use them as a guide for the decisions in rounds 4 and 5: each team answers in two lines before choosing price and channel, and reports the numbers in the decision memo.
