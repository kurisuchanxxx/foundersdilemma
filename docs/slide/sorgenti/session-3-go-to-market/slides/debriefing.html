Guiding questions

Five questions for rounds 4 and 5.

01

Do you have product-market fit? Which numbers prove it: retention, the Sean Ellis share, LTV/CAC?

02

Is it time to scale marketing, or would more spend just fill a leaky bucket?

03

Teams in the same sector share the same market: knowing that, how does your strategy change?

04

Did an outside competitor make a move in your sector? How did you respond?

05

Coopetition: is a partnership worth more with a team in your sector, or with one that has similar customers in another?

Session 3 · Go-to-market and unit economics

15

An example of linking back to theory, from the teacher’s guide: your CAC went up because the market moved past the early adopters and your PMF was below 55; that is the chasm. On coopetition (Brandenburger and Nalebuff): in the game a partnership lasts 3 rounds; between teams in the same sector it lowers CAC and churn (alliance), between different sectors it lowers CAC and boosts word of mouth (co-marketing).
