A case: bootstrapping

Mailchimp: twenty years without venture capital.

Intuit, 2021

$12 billion

in cash and stock, for a company that had never taken outside capital.

Founded in 2001 in Atlanta by Ben Chestnut and Dan Kurzius, it grew on customer revenue, with no investors.

In 2021 Intuit bought it: one of the largest exits ever for a company never funded by venture capital.

Bootstrapping is not just a phase:
it can be a strategy.

In the game. You can make it to the end without investors: one of the achievements to unlock is “Bootstrapped”.

Session 5 · Financing growth

04

Mailchimp, the email marketing service founded in 2001 in Atlanta, grew for twenty years on revenue alone, with no outside investors. In September 2021 Intuit announced it would acquire the company for about $12 billion in cash and stock; the deal closed on November 1, 2021 (sources: TechCrunch, September 13, 2021; Axios). The founders kept control and almost all of the ownership until the sale. Question for the class: what made it possible to grow without investors, and in which industries would it have been impossible?
