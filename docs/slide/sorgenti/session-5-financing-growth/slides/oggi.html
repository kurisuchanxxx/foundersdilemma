Today’s decisions.

Pitches: at most 2 per round, to investors with limited slots and capital.

Term sheets: valuation, equity, board seats and option pool.

Rescue round: when cash goes negative, at 30% of the valuation with a 2x preference.

Acquisitions between teams: cash offers, decided in the following round.

Session 5 · Financing growth

12

All of the round’s pitches, from every team and every bot, are evaluated together: each investor signs the best ones by interest until it runs out of slots and capital; the others get a rejection that names who got the money. A deal closes with an interest of at least 0.55 and an ask within 115% of the fair valuation; between 0.4 and 0.55 an alternative term sheet comes back. The rescue round covers the deficit plus 3 months of burn and adds a board seat; how many rescues are possible depends on the difficulty (3 in Forgiving, 1 in Standard, none in Realistic). An acquisition offer must be worth at least half of the other company’s valuation and cannot exceed the buyer’s cash.
