Lesson

Summary

Before you hire, five questions.

01

Do we need this job now, or does a company we are not yet need it?

02

Employee, contractor or advisor: what do we really need?

03

How much equity can we offer, and from which pool?

04

Who coordinates whom when the team doubles?

05

How many hours can we sustain for a year, not a month?

Session 4 · People and organization

10

The five questions sum up the lesson and apply to any real startup: the first two are about whom to hire and in what form, the third about the price in equity, the fourth about coordination as the team grows, the last about whether the founders’ workload is sustainable. Ask the teams to answer them before making offers to candidates in today’s round, and to bring the answers into the decision memo.
