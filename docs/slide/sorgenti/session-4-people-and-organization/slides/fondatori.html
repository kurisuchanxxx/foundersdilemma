What helps

Planned rest, not just rest after a crash.

Sharing the weight of decisions: co-founders, advisors, a mentor.

Talking about it: pressure grows when it stays hidden.

A long runway: the fear of running out of cash weighs more than the hours.

In the game. Stress above 0.85 for two months triggers burnout: founders at half capacity for 3 months. A vacation resets fatigue.

09

Michael Freeman and colleagues (The prevalence and co-occurrence of psychiatric conditions among entrepreneurs and their families, Small Business Economics, 2019) compared 242 entrepreneurs with a group of non-entrepreneurs: the entrepreneurs more often reported a history of depression (30%), ADHD (29%), substance use (12%) and bipolar disorder (11%). The data come from a questionnaire, not from diagnoses, but the message is clear: founder health is a company resource, not a private matter. The four practices are common-sense guidance, not findings of the study. Question for the class: who on your team would be the first to notice that a founder is at the limit?
