Debriefing

Guiding questions

Three questions for rounds 6 and 7.

01

Are you hiring people or contractors? Which choice is right at this stage?

02

Scarce talent: salary or equity? Which candidates preferred which, and why?

03

If you lost a candidate at auction, what would you have offered differently?

Session 4 · People and organization

12

On the second question: candidates who lean toward equity give more weight to the stake, and the equity they ask for changes with the company’s stage. On the third: an offer lost close to the threshold leaves a counter-offer valid for the next round, so the game is not always over. Connect the answers to morale and runway: a runway that is too short penalizes every offer.
