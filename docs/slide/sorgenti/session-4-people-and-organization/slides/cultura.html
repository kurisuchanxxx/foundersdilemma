Session 4 · People and organization

07

Culture is not what is written on the walls, but what the company rewards and what it tolerates. The line comes from the Netflix Culture: Freedom & Responsibility deck, published in 2009 by Reed Hastings with Patty McCord, then head of people: it became one of the most widely read documents in Silicon Valley, and Sheryl Sandberg called it perhaps the most important document ever to come out of the Valley. In the same deck Netflix points out that Enron also had beautiful values displayed in its lobby. In a startup, culture forms with the first people and the first hard decisions: who gets hired, who gets promoted, what gets forgiven. Question for the class: what behavior would your team never tolerate?
