Session 4 · People and organization

06

Brooks’s law comes from Fred Brooks’s experience managing IBM’s OS/360 operating system: adding manpower to a late software project makes it later, because newcomers have to be trained by the people already doing the work, and the coordination effort grows faster than the team. With n people, the pairs who need to talk to each other number n × (n - 1) ÷ 2. For a startup the lesson is that growing headcount is not free: it takes structure, clear ownership and small groups. Question for the class: at what headcount would your team need a first layer of managers?
