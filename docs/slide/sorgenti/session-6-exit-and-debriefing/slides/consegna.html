The Team report already contains numbers, charts and round-by-round decisions, so the final report can focus on analysis. In the Funding and control section, teams place themselves on the Rich or King chart and explain why; in the Reality check they pick a real case similar to their own trajectory and say where the game simplifies. There are five Lessons learned, each transferable to a real project. The rubric weighs data analysis and use of theory (25% each), quality of the post-mortem (20%), finance and control (15%) and clarity (15%).
