Guiding questions

Four questions about the whole journey.

01

If you got an acquisition offer today, at what price would you sell? Why?

02

If one team acquired another: who came out ahead, the buyer or the seller?

03

Which decision would you make differently, and what did it teach you?

04

Where does the game oversimplify reality?

Session 6 · Exit and final debriefing

12

For the first question, ask teams to answer with the waterfall in hand: which price leaves the founders an amount that makes selling worthwhile? For the second, the guide suggests having teams tell the story of the negotiation: who proposed, who accepted and why, and whether the price was fair given the sellers’ waterfall and the buyer’s cash. The fourth question is a great prompt for assessing critical thinking: the next slide lists the simplifications the model declares.
