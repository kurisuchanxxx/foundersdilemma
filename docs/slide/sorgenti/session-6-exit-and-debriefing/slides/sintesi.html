Five ideas to take with you.

01

A startup is searching for a business model; the market matters more than the idea.

02

Product-market fit is measured, not declared.

03

A customer has to be worth more than it costs to acquire.

04

Every euro you raise costs equity or control: rich or king.

05

The price of an exit is not what the founders take home.
