Session 6 · Exit and final debriefing

07

Many founders write a post-mortem when their company shuts down: these are exactly the texts CB Insights drew on for the analysis of why startups fail that you saw in session 1. A good post-mortem separates facts from interpretations, reconstructs each decision with the information available at the time (not with what you know today), and tells apart what was up to the team from what was not. Annie Duke (Thinking in Bets, 2018) calls resulting the tendency to judge a decision by its result: a reasonable choice can end badly through bad luck, and a reckless one can turn out well. In the final report, the Three key decisions section asks for exactly this: context, alternatives, choice, outcome and what you would do again. Question for the class: which of your decisions went well by luck?
