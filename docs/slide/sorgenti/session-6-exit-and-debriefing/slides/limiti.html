Limits and simplifications

Where the game simplifies.

Simplified cash

No taxes, VAT, working capital or depreciation.

Bare-bones term sheets

Only 1x or 2x non-participating preferences; no anti-dilution, drag-along or secondary sales.

Stylized competitors

Moves at the industry level, with no customers or prices of their own.

Rigid deals

Partnerships with fixed effects; cash-only acquisitions, with no earn-out.

Fixed market

Its size changes only through events.

People as numbers

Few skills; culture and conflict boiled down to morale, stress and dilemmas.
