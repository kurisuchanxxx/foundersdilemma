The market

“When a great team meets a lousy market, market wins.
When a lousy team meets a great market, market wins.

When a great team meets a great market,
something special happens.”

Andy Rachleff, quoted by Marc Andreessen in The only thing that matters (2007)

In the game. The sector sets market size, willingness to pay, churn and starting margin. You choose it on day one.

Session 1 · The founding team and the idea

06

Andy Rachleff, co-founder of Benchmark Capital, in the version Marc Andreessen made famous: of a startup’s three ingredients (team, product, market), the market matters most. A market that pulls will lift even a mediocre product; a market that is not there will sink even the best team. Question for the class: think of a well-made product that did not succeed. Was it the team or the market?
