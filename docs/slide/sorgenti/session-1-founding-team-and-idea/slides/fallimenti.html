Session 1 · The founding team and the idea

05

CB Insights read post-mortems written by the founders themselves: the top cause is not a lack of money but having built something nobody wanted. Running out of cash is often the consequence, not the cause. For a sense of scale: according to research by Shikhar Ghosh (Harvard Business School, 2012), about three in four venture-backed startups do not return investors’ capital. Question for the class: which of these causes can you test before spending much?
