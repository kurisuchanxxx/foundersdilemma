The equity split is the decision founders make fastest and the one they renegotiate most often. Thomas Hellmann and Noam Wasserman (The First Deal: The Division of Founder Equity in New Ventures, Management Science, 2016) find that about a third of teams split equally, and that equal splits decided right away, on a handshake, are associated with lower valuations in the first round: investors read them as a sign of difficult conversations avoided. Quick exercise: each team writes down its split and one sentence that justifies it.
