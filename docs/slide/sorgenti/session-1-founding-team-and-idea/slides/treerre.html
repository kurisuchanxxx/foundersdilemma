Lesson

Noam Wasserman, The Founder’s Dilemmas (2012)

Three choices to make before anything else.

Wasserman studied nearly 10,000 founders: the choices of the first months weigh on the company for its whole life.

Relationships

Who to found with: friends, colleagues or strangers, similar or complementary skills.

Roles

Who does what and who decides, starting with who will be CEO.

Rewards

How to split the equity, with what vesting, with what salaries.

In the game. At founding, teams choose founder archetypes, the equity split and vesting: changing them later is costly.

Wasserman’s “three Rs” (Relationships, Roles, Rewards) are the decisions founders make first, often in a hurry and without discussing them, and that later turn into the causes of conflict. The point of the lesson is that there is no choice that is right in absolute terms, but each one has costs worth knowing in advance. The next three slides take them one at a time.
