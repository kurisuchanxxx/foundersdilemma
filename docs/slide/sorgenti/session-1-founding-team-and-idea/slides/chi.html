Session 1 · The founding team and the idea

09

Complementary skills reduce risk: the person who builds the product and the person who finds the customers face two different uncertainties. Wasserman observes that teams formed from friendships or family ties are comfortable at first but more fragile when the hard decisions arrive (roles, equity, departures). Ron Wayne’s story shows the other side: not every co-founder has the same tolerance for risk, and it is better to find out right away. Question for the class: on your team, who builds, who sells, who decides?
