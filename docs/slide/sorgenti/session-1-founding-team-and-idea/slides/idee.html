Session 1 · The founding team and the idea

07

Paul Graham, founder of Y Combinator: “The way to get startup ideas is not to try to think of startup ideas. It’s to look for problems, preferably problems you have yourself.” Ideas dreamed up at a desk tend to sound plausible and interest no one. In all three cases the problem belonged to the founders themselves, and in Slack’s case the solution came out of a failed project. Question for the class: what problem did you run into this week that nobody solves well?
